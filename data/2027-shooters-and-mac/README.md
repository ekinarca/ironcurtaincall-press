# Upcoming shooters and Mac games of 2027 on Steam (dataset)

Two dated lists of games with a 2027 date on Steam, kept by Whale Development Studios, the maker of Iron Curtain Call, which is
one row on both lists (marked `made_by_the_dataset_publisher`). Files: `fps2027.csv` / `fps2027.json` (64 games) and
`mac2027.csv` / `mac2027.json` (64 games). The pages that show them: https://ironcurtaincall.com/guides/upcoming-fps-games-2027/ and
https://ironcurtaincall.com/guides/upcoming-mac-games-2027/

- **Source:** the US Steam store (store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails with cc=us for the date, platforms and demo; Steam's own
  search lists for the candidates). Developer and publisher as Steam prints them. `steam_tags_as_style` are the first matching
  Steam tags, in Steam's order, in English.
- **Read on:** 2026-11-07 (both lists, every row read again that morning). Dates move, so the store page always has the final word.
- **Dates:** `release_as_steam_shows_it` is a day (2027-03-11), a month (2027-03), a quarter (2027-Q1) or the year (2027), exactly as precise as
  Steam is; `release_precision` says which.
- **Rules:** fps2027: A game (not a DLC, demo or soundtrack) whose Steam page shows a 2027 date, with the FPS or Boomer Shooter tag among its first six tags and none of those six naming another kind of game (simulation, party, tower defense, puzzle, idle, card, strategy, management, sports, racing and the like), and no adult only content. Every such game with a day or a month is in. Of those with only a quarter or the year: the boomer shooters that name a quarter, the shooters in the part of Steam's FPS "Popular Upcoming" list that is ranked by popularity (its first 415 places on the day), and two big studio games whose tags put the story ahead of the shooting: Squadron 42 and Clockwork Revolution.
  mac2027: A game whose Steam page shows a 2027 date and lists macOS, with none of its first six tags naming another kind of game (as above, and also RTS, MMORPG, walking simulator) and no adult only content. Every shooter (a shooter tag among its first five tags) is in; other action games (an action tag among their first three) are in when they sit in the popularity ranked part of Steam's Mac "Popular Upcoming" lists for the Action and Shooter tags (the first 482 and 300 places on the day).
- **This is not the full 2027 calendar.** Steam has many more games with a 2027 date; these are the shooters and the Mac shooters and
  action games that meet the rules above. No game is ranked.
- **Updated** monthly with the pages; the pages' own date says when. Regenerated by `node guides/export-datasets.mjs` in the site repository.
